Frequently Asked Questions

How can I update my profile?
Go to Account Settings, update your information and click "Save Changes".
How can I change my password?
Open Password settings, enter your current password and create a new password.
How do I contact ConnectHub support?
Use the support form below and describe your problem clearly.
Why are my messages not appearing?
Make sure the server is running and that you have selected a user before sending a message.
