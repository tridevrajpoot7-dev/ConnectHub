Help & Support ❓

Find answers and get help with ConnectHub.

How do I edit my profile?

Open Settings → Account or use Edit Profile from your profile.

How do I create a post?

Go to Home, write your post and click Create Post.

How do I change privacy?

Open Settings → Privacy and switch between Public and Private.
